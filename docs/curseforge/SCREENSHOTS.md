is real generated terrain with posed mobs: frozen (no AI), silent, invulnerable, and they stay put. Paste the teleport
command; it puts you on the scene's redstone block with the exact facing. Time and weather don't matter in the nether.
Shaders on, F1 to hide the HUD. Save each as the name below; they get converted to JPEG under 2 MB afterwards.
